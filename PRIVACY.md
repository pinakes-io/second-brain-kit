# Privacy notice (draft)

This is orientation in plain language. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

## What this kit is

A set of text files: instructions, skills and templates, plus small checking scripts. There is no server, no account, no tracking and no analytics. The author of the kit does not receive any of your data.

## Where your data goes

- **Your documents** are created in **your own Google Drive**. They stay there until you move or delete them.
- **What you type into the chat and the documents the assistant reads** are processed by the assistant's provider (Anthropic, for claude.ai) and, for the Drive connection, by Google. That happens under their terms and your account settings, not under this kit. Check the privacy and data settings of both accounts yourself, for example whether conversations may be used for model training, how long they are kept, and whether memory is switched on.
- **Chat memory:** the kit does not rely on it. Everything that matters is written to a document that you can read and change.

## What the kit does to limit risk

- ID, account, tax and insurance numbers are **always blocked**: the assistant does not ask for them and does not store them.
- You can block more topics in the interview: health information, information about children, names of other people, amounts of money.
- The assistant is told to delete nothing, share nothing and send nothing in your name without a clear yes.
- The repository contains only invented test people.
- The instructions ask you to try the kit first with a separate test account.

## What you should do

- Do not put passwords, keys or full ID, bank or tax numbers into the chat or into documents.
- Use a Google account (or at least a Drive) that holds no documents other people shared with you. Text hidden in such a document could steer the assistant.
- A chat feels private, but the assistant is not a person and the provider processes what you type. Think before you share sensitive details, and remember that you can always say "skip".
- If someone helps you with the setup, store their role ("partner", "friend"), not their name.
- If you store details about children or health, think about who else can open your Drive and check your sharing settings.
- Treat everything the assistant writes as a draft. Check numbers, deadlines and rules against an official source before you act on them.

## No advice

The kit gives no legal, tax or medical advice.

## Questions

Open an issue without personal data. Do not post screenshots that show your documents.
